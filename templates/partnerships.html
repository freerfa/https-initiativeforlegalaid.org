What partners can expect from ILA

Strong Institutional Commitment

A steadfast commitment to justice, human rights, the rule of law, democratic governance, and ethical service delivery.

Professional Legal Expertise

A multidisciplinary team of qualified legal practitioners, human rights specialists, governance experts, researchers, programme managers, and development professionals.

Deep Contextual Understanding

Comprehensive knowledge of South Sudan's legal framework, governance environment, cultural diversity, conflict dynamics, and community realities, enabling context-sensitive interventions.

Community-Centred Programming

Strong relationships with local communities and stakeholders that ensure programmes are participatory, inclusive, responsive, and sustainable.

Transparency and Accountability

Robust governance, financial management, procurement, safeguarding, and accountability systems that promote responsible stewardship of resources and compliance with donor requirements.

Strategic Partnerships

Collaboration with government institutions, civil society organizations, community structures, and international partners that enhances programme effectiveness and collective impact.

Innovation and Continuous Learning

Commitment to research, evidence generation, digital innovation, adaptive programming, and organizational learning that strengthens programme quality and long-term impact.

Effective Project Management

Strong systems for project planning, implementation, monitoring, evaluation, financial reporting, risk management, and results-based management.

Operational Flexibility

Ability to design and implement programmes across diverse geographic, political, and humanitarian contexts, including remote, conflict-affected, and hard-to-reach areas.

Focus on Sustainable Impact

A long-term commitment to strengthening local institutions, building community resilience, promoting local ownership, and delivering measurable, lasting development outcomes.

Donor information

Due diligence and reporting

ILA maintains governance, financial management, procurement, safeguarding and accountability systems designed to meet donor compliance requirements and to support responsible stewardship of the resources entrusted to it. The Board of Directors operates a Finance & Audit Committee and a Risk & Compliance Committee, and an Internal Audit Officer reports independently of programme management.

Institutional documentation — governance, financial, safeguarding and MEAL — is available to prospective donors on request from the head office in Juba.

  • Policy dialogue and legal reform
  • Institutional capacity development
  • Knowledge sharing and learning
  • Resource mobilisation
