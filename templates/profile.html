The organisation

A professional platform for justice, rights and accountable institutions

The Initiative for Legal Aid (ILA) is an independent, non-profit, non-governmental, and non-political civil society organization established to advance access to justice, promote and protect human rights, strengthen the rule of law, and contribute to democratic governance and sustainable peace throughout the Republic of South Sudan.

The organization serves as a professional platform for legal assistance, policy advocacy, institutional strengthening, and community empowerment, working to ensure that justice is accessible to all, particularly vulnerable and marginalized populations.

Our history

Established in 2021

ILA was created in recognition of the urgent need for accessible legal services and stronger justice institutions in South Sudan — a country that continues to face significant legal and governance challenges arising from years of conflict, institutional limitations, poverty, displacement, inequality, and limited access to justice services.

Since its establishment, the organization has been dedicated to addressing legal inequalities, promoting peaceful dispute resolution, empowering vulnerable communities through legal education, and supporting institutional reforms that strengthen democratic governance and the rule of law.

ILA was founded on the conviction that sustainable peace, national development, and social justice can only be achieved where justice is accessible to every citizen and public institutions uphold the rights and dignity of all people.

Our philosophy

Why legal empowerment matters

ILA believes that justice is the foundation upon which peaceful societies, democratic governance, sustainable development, and human dignity are built. Every human being is born with inherent rights and deserves equal protection before the law without discrimination.

We recognize that poverty, inequality, conflict, discrimination, weak institutions, and limited legal awareness continue to prevent many people from accessing justice. ILA is therefore committed to ensuring that legal services, legal knowledge, and justice institutions become accessible to everyone — particularly those who are vulnerable, marginalized, and historically excluded from formal justice systems.

Our approach

How ILA works

A comprehensive, rights-based, people-centred, community-driven and partnership-oriented approach to justice, human rights and democratic governance.

Human Rights-Based Programming

All programmes are grounded in internationally recognized human rights principles, ensuring equality, participation, accountability, transparency, non-discrimination, and respect for human dignity.

People-Centred Service Delivery

The needs, priorities, experiences, and aspirations of beneficiaries remain at the centre of programme design and implementation. We deliver accessible, responsive, culturally appropriate, and high-quality services.

Community Participation and Ownership

Communities are active partners rather than passive beneficiaries. We promote inclusive participation by engaging women, youth, persons with disabilities, traditional leaders, and local authorities throughout the programme cycle.

Legal Empowerment

ILA equips individuals and communities with the legal knowledge, practical skills, and confidence needed to understand their rights, claim legal remedies, resolve disputes peacefully, and participate effectively in governance processes.

Institutional Strengthening

Recognizing that effective justice depends on strong institutions, we provide technical support, organizational development, governance strengthening, and capacity-building to justice institutions, civil society organizations, and community structures.

Strategic Partnerships

Collaborative action generates greater impact than isolated efforts. We work closely with government institutions, development partners, civil society, academia, traditional authorities, and international organizations.

Governance

Independent, transparent and accountable

ILA is governed by its Constitution, internal policies, and applicable national legislation, and is committed to transparency, accountability, good governance, and compliance with all legal and regulatory requirements governing civil society organizations. The organization functions independently of political parties, religious institutions, commercial interests, or governmental control.
